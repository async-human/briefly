What makes it different

Not a summarizer.
A second brain.

The difference between a tool you abandon in two weeks and one you can't start the day without.

🎯
Understands you, specifically
Every item explains why it matters to you — your role, your goals, your history. Not why it's generally important. The same story looks different in your briefing than in anyone else's.
OpenAI launched a new model with improved reasoning.
Briefly: "As someone building an agent pipeline, the 40% improvement in tool-use reliability changes how much you can trust multi-step chains without human oversight."
🔗
Memory across days
Briefly remembers what it showed you. Every update references the original story. No more "wait, I read something about this last week..." moments.
📚
All your sources, one place
Newsletters forwarded by email. RSS feeds, YouTube channels, subreddits — all ingested, all synthesized. Connect once, read once.
🚫
Confident filtering
Briefly doesn't just summarize everything. It confidently decides what not to show — and tells you exactly what it skipped and why.
💬
Ask follow-up questions
Reply to any item: "tell me more," "what are the counterarguments," "find everything I've read about this topic." Your knowledge base, searchable.
📎
Citations, always
Every claim links to its source. Every story cites every publication that covered it. You can always verify, always go deeper. No hallucinations.
